How to Play

Marvel Champions is a co-operative card game: you are a hero, the game runs the villain, and you win or lose together.

Guided first game

Starts Spider-Man vs Rhino from the Core Set — the game's own starter matchup — with a coach that explains each part of the game the first time you meet it. You can stop the hints at any point without stopping the game.

Winning and losing

  • You win when the villain is defeated. Damage it until its hit points are gone; it then advances to its next stage with a fresh, larger pool of hit points, until there are no stages left.
  • You lose if the main scheme fills with threat, or if your hero is defeated by taking damage equal to their hit points.

Two forms, one hero

Your identity card has two sides and you may flip it once per turn.

Hero form Alter-ego form
Attack, thwart and defend. The villain attacks you. Recover damage and a larger hand size. The villain schemes instead, adding threat to the main scheme.

Choosing when to flip down and heal is the central decision of the game.

The round

  1. Player phase. Each player takes a turn: play cards, use abilities, and use basic powers. Your hero card exhausts (turns sideways) when it uses a basic power, so you get one per turn.
    • Attack — deal damage equal to your ATK to an enemy.
    • Thwart — remove threat equal to your THW from a scheme.
    • Defend — offered when an enemy attacks you; reduces the damage by your DEF.
    • Recover — alter-ego only; heal damage equal to your REC.
    At the end of your turn you draw back up to your hand size.
  2. Villain phase, in four steps:
    1. The main scheme gains threat — its escalation value per player, plus any acceleration icons in play.
    2. The villain activates against each player, and every engaged minion activates against the player it is engaged with. Attacks flip a face-down boost card that can add to the damage.
    3. Each player is dealt one encounter card face down.
    4. Those cards are revealed and resolved.

Paying for cards

Every player card costs resources. You pay by discarding other cards from your hand: each card is worth the resource icon printed in its corner. Cards, upgrades and your alter-ego side can generate resources too.

Controls

Playing a card or using a power

  1. Click the card — in your hand, or your hero card for its basic powers.
  2. If it offers more than one ability, pick one from the buttons that appear.
  3. Click the legal targets, which are highlighted.
  4. Click cards in hand to pay the cost.
  5. Press OK to confirm.

The End button on the right edge ends your turn. The sidebar tucked against the right edge holds Log, Pause, Undo, Redo and QSave.
